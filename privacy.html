Privacy Policy – Mr & Mrs Game

Effective Date: 25/10/2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personally identifiable information (PII):

  • Name
  • Email address

This information is collected when you sign up, participate in multiplayer features, or contact us directly.

If you leave Share usage data switched on, we also collect product-usage events: which screen you opened, and whether you signed in, created a game, saved questions, sent an invite, started or answered a game, revealed results, completed a game, or purchased, and whether one of those steps (or a password reset) failed. Those events use fixed labels (for example a screen name, or “email” versus “apple”), yes/no flags, and coarse buckets such as “1–5 questions”. They are linked only to your account id.

We do not send your name, email address, question text, answers, photos, videos, media links, access codes, or game identifiers in these events. The analytics SDK may attach basic technical context such as app version, device model, and operating system. You can turn this off at any time in the app under Account → Share usage data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to game features
  • To send updates or notifications (with your consent)
  • To respond to inquiries or support requests
  • To personalize your experience
  • To understand how the app is used so we can improve it (usage analytics, which you can switch off under Account)

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We store your game and account information only as long as necessary. You may delete your account in the app (Account → Delete Account) or by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your authentication record and game data.

Usage-analytics events are stored separately by our analytics provider and are linked only to a pseudonymous account id. In-app account deletion does not remove those events. Email us and we will delete them on request. Otherwise they are kept for the provider’s data-retention period.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services to enhance your experience:

  • Supabase (authentication and game data) — supabase.com/privacy
  • Apple and Google (sign-in, and the app stores)
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchases; we do not store your card details) — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog (product analytics, data stored in the EU) — posthog.com/privacy

Usage events are sent to PostHog’s EU cloud (eu.i.posthog.com). We do not enable session replay, and we ask PostHog not to derive your location from your IP address. These services may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to protect it. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including by switching off Share usage data in Account

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below. Ask us specifically if you want usage-analytics events deleted as well as your account.

6. Children’s Privacy

This game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, it will be deleted immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. All changes will be posted here. Continued use of the app after any changes implies acceptance of the new terms.
